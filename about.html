About Pawsport Concierge — International Pet Travel, Handled

Pawsport Concierge is a Miami-based pet travel concierge that helps pet parents travel and relocate internationally with their dogs and cats. We manage the documentation, airline coordination, and country entry requirements behind every trip — from health certificates to airport procedures.

PAWSPORT · MIA

It Started With One Dog and a Lot of Paperwork

Pawsport Concierge began long before it became a company.

Our founder spent years traveling the world — more than 50 countries, often with her Maltipoo, Nounours. Traveling with a pet quickly taught her that getting from one country to another is rarely as simple as booking a ticket. Every destination came with its own rules: health certificates, airline requirements, entry permits, vaccination timelines, airport procedures. And often, different answers depending on who you asked.

Over years of trial, error, and repetition, she built real experience, trusted contacts, and a practical understanding of how international pet travel actually works.

Eventually, friends started asking for help. A dog moving from the U.S. to Europe. A family taking their pet on vacation. Someone relocating internationally with a cat. Someone who had already booked a flight and suddenly discovered a missing document. One friend introduced another, and what started as favors gradually became a full-service pet travel concierge.

What Pawsport Concierge Does

We coordinate every detail that makes international pet travel possible:

Pet travel documentation

Health certificates, entry permits, and country-specific paperwork, prepared on the right timeline.

Airline coordination

Booking requirements, crate and carrier rules, and direct communication with the airline.

Destination entry rules

Vaccination timelines, import requirements, and quarantine regulations for your specific route.

Airport procedures

What happens at check-in, security, and arrival, so nothing surprises you on travel day.

Instead of spending weeks sorting through conflicting information and calling airlines, veterinary offices, and government agencies, our clients have one point of contact guiding the process from start to finish.

We know what it feels like to stand at an airport with a pet you love and wonder: “Did we get everything right?” Our job is to make sure the answer is yes.

Who We Help

Pawsport Concierge works with pet parents flying with dogs and cats to and from the United States — whether you’re going on vacation, relocating overseas for work, or bringing a pet home from abroad. Based in Miami, we assist clients traveling through Miami International Airport and other major U.S. hubs, with destination expertise across Europe, Latin America, and beyond.
