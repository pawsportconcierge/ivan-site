2.2What we are not. We are not an airline or other carrier, an indirect air carrier, a freight forwarder, a customs broker, a travel agent or seller of travel, a veterinarian or veterinary practice, a laboratory, a pharmacy, a government agency, an insurer, or a law firm. We do not give veterinary, medical, legal, immigration, customs, or tax advice. Information we give you about requirements is practical coordination guidance based on the sources available to us at the time. It is not a legal opinion or a veterinary recommendation.

2.3Tickets and reservations. We do not sell or issue airline tickets, cargo space, or accommodation, and we do not buy them for you. You buy these directly from the airline or other provider, in your own name and at your own cost. Pet travel fees and other Third-Party Costs we may pay for you with your approval under Section 7.3. If we help you make or change a reservation, we act only on your written instruction and as your assistant, and the booking is a contract between you and that provider (Section 5).

2.4Air transport. We do not transport animals by air. While your Pet is in the care of an airline or its handling agents, your Pet is subject only to that airline’s terms, conditions, and limits of liability. Unless your Order expressly says otherwise, you or a person you authorize will hand your Pet over to the airline and collect your Pet from the airline.

2.5Scope and Change Orders. The Services include only the tasks listed in your Order. Any other work requires a written agreement, which may be made by email or messaging, stating the additional work and any additional fee (a “Change Order”). Work caused by a change in your plans, travel dates, destination, or Pet, or by a change in regulations or airline policy after the date of your Order, is additional work. Before we begin additional work that carries an additional fee, we will tell you the fee in writing and wait for your approval, unless a regulatory or airline deadline requires us to act sooner.

2.6Timelines and cost figures. Timelines, processing times, and third-party cost figures we provide are good-faith estimates based on the information available to us. Actual times and costs are set by the providers concerned.

2.7Everyday pet services. When we collect or deliver prescriptions, food, documents, or other items, we act only as your courier and coordinator. We do not prescribe, dispense, or advise on medication. You are responsible for checking any medication or product before use.

2.8Our personnel. “Our personnel” means our members, managers, employees, and the individual contractors we engage to perform our own part of the Services. Third-Party Providers (Section 13) are not our personnel.

Treatment decisions are made by the veterinarian, and we are not responsible for them. We will never consent to euthanasia of your Pet on your behalf.

10.5Hand-over and collection. You, or the contact named in your Order, will be available at the agreed time and place to hand over and collect your Pet.
